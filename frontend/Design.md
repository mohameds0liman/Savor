## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, sensory indulgence, and refined craft of modern home cooking and gastronomic storytelling. It merges the tactility and editorial elegance of contemporary culinary magazines with the precision and responsiveness of modern digital lifestyle apps.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Appetizing & Welcoming:** Emits gentle culinary heat through roasted earth tones, sun-warmed spices, and crisp herbal accents.
- **Editorial & Measured:** Balances evocative serif headlines with clean, uncluttered negative space to let food imagery take center stage.
- **Thoughtful & Accessible:** Clear interactions, forgiving tap regions, high legibility, and effortless organization designed for messy counter setups and relaxed meal planning alike.

### Design Movement: Warm Culinary Editorial
A blend of warm minimalism and editorial publication style. It relies on rich cream backdrops instead of sterile pure whites, subtle tinted borders, deliberate typographic hierarchy, and soft atmospheric shadows that anchor cards with organic physical presence.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from the kitchen: sun-dried spices, toasted grains, crisp garden herbs, and deep espresso tones.

### Palette Tokens & Application
- **Primary (`#D9532F` / Deep Tone `#C84620`):** Rich paprika and terracotta. Reserved for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, interactive favorites, and vital navigation anchors.
- **Secondary (`#EAA844`):** Toasted saffron and warm amber. Used for secondary highlights, rating stars, cooking status accents, and celebration badges.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#4E7958`):** Fresh herb sage and rosemary. Represents dietary tags (vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free), ingredient freshness, positive validation, and nutritional cues.
- **Neutral Dark / Charcoal (`#221C19`):** Deep espresso slate. Replaces harsh blacks for primary typography, icons, and grounded structural elements.
- **Neutral Muted (`#6E645E`):** Warm pecan gray. Used for metadata, secondary body copy, cooking timing labels, and subtle iconography.
- **Neutral Light / Surfaces:**
  - Base Canvas: Soft Cream / Warm Linen (`#FDFBF7`)
  - Elevated Container / Card Surface: Pure Milk Linen (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Subtle Dividing Border: Warm Oat Linen (`#EBE5DF`)
  - Focus Ring: Terracotta Glow (`rgba(217, 83, 47, 0.25)`)

## Typography

Typography establishes an appetizing narrative hierarchy. Headlines invite discovery through classic serif flair, while body instructions and data metrics remain legible in dynamic cooking environments.

- **Headline Hierarchy (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for page banners, story headers, and recipe titles. Use italic variants sparingly for editorial flavor (e.g., cooking techniques, subheadings, or chef notes).
- **Body & Label Hierarchy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Applied to ingredients, cooking instructions, dietary chips, timestamps, and interface controls. Offers open counters, friendly proportions, and clear numeric distinctions for measurements (e.g., fractions, cooking times).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system resting inside a max-width container of `75rem` (1200px) with generous horizontal padding to evoke wide magazine margins.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 32px (`2rem`) gutters, dynamic safe margins expanding with viewport. Recipe grids default to 3 or 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 32px margin. Recipe grids adapt to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px margin. Recipe grids collapse to a single column or an edge-to-edge horizontal snap carousel for curated collections.

### Spacing Strategy
Spacing utilizes an 8pt base grid with a 4pt sub-grid for tight micro-alignments (e.g., icons next to prep times, badge insets). Structural sections leverage expansive margins (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish rhythm and provide visual breathing room between curated lists and featured stories.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system favors tonal hierarchy and warm, diffused ambient shadows over stark drop shadows. Depth mimics the physical layering of fine parchment, recipe cards, and linen tablecloths.

### Elevation Levels
- **Base Level (`Surface 0`):** Soft Cream canvas (`#FDFBF7`) without shadows.
- **Cards & Tiles (`Elevation 1`):** Pure white container (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate warm hairline outline (`1px solid #EBE5DF`) and an ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(34, 28, 25, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(34, 28, 25, 0.03);`
- **Interactive Card Hover (`Elevation 2`):** Elevates slightly upward with enriched warm diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(34, 28, 25, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(34, 28, 25, 0.04);`
  `transform: translateY(-3px); transition: all 240ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1);`
- **Sticky Navigation & Modals (`Elevation 3`):** Semi-translucent warm cream glass with backdrop filter:
  `background: rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid #EBE5DF;`

## Shapes

The shape language reflects organic culinary contours. Elements feature a balanced roundedness of 0.5rem (`8px`) on base inputs and controls, stepping up to 1rem (`16px`) for cards and containers, and fully rounded pills for interactive chips and tags.

- **Inputs, Buttons & Small Components:** `0.5rem` (8px). Grounded yet inviting.
- **Recipe Cards & Content Containers:** `1rem` (16px) with masked media overflow to maintain continuous soft corners.
- **Badges, Filters & Dietary Chips:** Pill shape (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from rectangular structural containers.
- **Favorite Floating Action Buttons:** Circular (`rounded-full`) to complement the heart glyph.

## Components

### 1. Sticky Top Navigation
- **Structure:** Left-aligned editorial brand logo, centered navigation links (`Home`, `Favourites`, `My Recipes`, `Contact`), and right-aligned quick search bar paired with auth/profile avatar.
- **Styling:** Frosted Linen background (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.9)` with 12px blur) bordered by `#EBE5DF` at the base.
- **Search Bar:** Integrated input with magnifying icon, soft background (`#FFFFFF`), subtle placeholder text in `#6E645E`, and a terracotta focus ring.
- **Active Navigation Indicator:** Subtle 2px bottom border or warm terracotta pill fill with soft transition.

### 2. Recipe Grid & Cards
- **Card Structure:** 
  - Media container with standardized `4:3` or `16:10` aspect ratio featuring lazy-loaded imagery and smooth zoom on hover (`scale(1.03)`).
  - Floating pill badges: Dietary tag on top-left (e.g., Herb Sage `#4E7958` background with white text), Heart toggle on top-right.
  - Body section: Recipe title in `Playfair Display`, followed by a secondary row showcasing Prep & Cook time (clock icon + text) and difficulty tier.
- **Favorite Heart Micro-Animation:**
  - Inactive: Frosted circular button (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)`), heart icon outlined in `#221C19`.
  - Active / Hover: Spring animation scaling to `1.2` then settling to `1.0`, heart filled with `#D9532F`.
  - Particle burst: Subtle micro-scale ring burst during selection.

### 3. Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Categorical Chips (Prep Time, Difficulty):** Warm Linen background (`#F4EFEA`), neutral text (`#6E645E`), non-clickable.
- **Dietary Badges (Vegan, GF, Nut-Free):** Tinted Sage background (`rgba(78, 121, 88, 0.12)`), solid Sage text (`#385A40`), uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** Bordered by `#EBE5DF`, transitioning to terracotta fill (`#D9532F`) with white text when selected.

### 4. Form Controls (Auth & Contact)
- **Inputs & Textareas:** Pure white background, `1px solid #EBE5DF`, `0.5rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#D9532F` accompanied by `0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 83, 47, 0.2)`.
- **Validation:** 
  - Error: Coral red stroke (`#C83220`) with helper icon and inline message.
  - Valid: Sage green stroke (`#4E7958`) with subtle checkmark accessory.

### 5. Buttons
- **Primary:** Terracotta background (`#D9532F`), white bold text, gentle lift on hover, active state `#C84620`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Warm white background, border `1.5px solid #221C19`, neutral text, turns to solid `#221C19` with cream text on hover.
- **Ghost:** Transparent canvas, muted neutral text, subtle background tint (`rgba(34, 28, 25, 0.05)`) on hover.

### 6. Tabbed Controls & Empty/Active Collection States
- **Tabs:** Segmented warm cream track with an animated active sliding pill in pure white with elevation. Text transitions from `#6E645E` to `#221C19` font-weight `600`.
- **Empty States:** Warm illustrative linework icon (e.g., empty mixing bowl or whisk), `headline-md` title, supportive body description, and a primary CTA inviting users to explore or create recipes.
